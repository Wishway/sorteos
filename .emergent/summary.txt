        *   A minimum purchase quantity per transaction can be set by the admin.
    *   **Ticket Management:**
        *   Tickets purchased via transfer are  until approved by the admin.
        *   Pending tickets are reserved for 24 hours and automatically expire (become available again) if not approved.
        *   Admin must enter a bank confirmation number to approve a ticket.
    *   **Admin Panel:**
        *   Dashboard for managing users (block/delete), raffles (create/edit/delete with rules), and commissions.
        *   Section to manage bank details and a WhatsApp contact number for payment confirmations.
        *   A view to approve/reject pending tickets.
        *   A view to see all approved tickets, filterable by raffle and ticket number.
    *   **User Panel:**
        *   A My Profile section to view personal data.
        *   A detailed history of purchased tickets, including raffle name, raffle ID, ticket status, and confirmation number.
